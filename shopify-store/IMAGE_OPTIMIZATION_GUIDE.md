# Product Image Optimization Guide

High-quality images = More sales! Follow this guide to optimize your product photos.

---

## Image Requirements for Shopify

### Technical Specs:
- **Format:** JPG or PNG (JPG for photos, PNG for graphics/transparent backgrounds)
- **Minimum Size:** 2048 x 2048 pixels
- **Recommended Size:** 2048 x 2048 pixels (square) or 2048 x 2560 (4:5 ratio)
- **Maximum File Size:** 4096 x 4096 pixels / 20 MB
- **File Size:** Keep under 500KB for fast loading (200-300KB ideal)
- **Color Mode:** RGB (not CMYK)

---

## How Many Images Per Product?

**Minimum:** 3 images
**Recommended:** 5-8 images
**Maximum:** Up to 250 images (but 8-10 is optimal)

### Image Order Matters:
1. **Main Image** - Clean, white background, front view
2. **Second Image** - Different angle or lifestyle shot
3. **Third Image** - Close-up of key feature
4. **Fourth Image** - Product in use
5. **Fifth Image** - Size/scale reference
6. **Sixth Image** - Additional angles
7. **Seventh Image** - Packaging/what's included
8. **Eighth Image** - Infographic with features

---

## Types of Product Photos You Need

### 1. White Background Photos (Essential)
- Clean, professional look
- No shadows or distractions
- Product centered
- High contrast
- **Use for:** Main product image

### 2. Lifestyle Photos (Highly Recommended)
- Product being used in real life
- Shows scale and context
- Creates emotional connection
- **Use for:** Secondary images

### 3. Detail Shots (Important)
- Close-ups of materials, textures
- Show quality and craftsmanship
- Highlight unique features
- **Use for:** 3rd-5th images

### 4. 360° Views (Optional but Great)
- Multiple angles
- Front, back, sides, top
- Helps customers understand product
- **Use for:** Additional images

### 5. Infographics (Recommended)
- Features and benefits overlay
- Dimensions and measurements
- How-to-use instructions
- **Use for:** Last 1-2 images

## Free Tools to Optimize Images

### Image Compression (Reduce file size):
1. **TinyPNG.com** - Best for PNG & JPG (FREE)
   - Reduces file size by 70% without quality loss
   - Drag & drop up to 20 images

2. **Squoosh.app** - Google's image optimizer (FREE)
   - Advanced compression settings
   - Compare before/after

3. **ImageOptim** - Mac app (FREE)
   - Batch process multiple images
   - Removes metadata

### Background Removal (Free):
1. **Remove.bg** - AI background remover (FREE)
   - 1-click white background
   - High quality results

2. **Canva** - Background remover (FREE with account)
   - Also has design tools

### Image Editing (Free):
1. **Canva.com** - Easy graphic design (FREE)
   - Create infographics
   - Add text overlays
   - Resize images

2. **Photopea.com** - Free Photoshop alternative
   - Edit in browser
   - Advanced features

3. **GIMP** - Free desktop software
   - Powerful editing tools

## Image Naming Best Practices

### Bad Names:
❌ IMG_1234.jpg
❌ photo.jpg
❌ untitled.png

### Good Names:
✅ blue-wireless-headphones-front.jpg
✅ leather-wallet-open-view.jpg
✅ yoga-mat-rolled-purple.jpg

### Format:
`[color]-[product-type]-[angle/feature].jpg`

**Why?**
- Helps with SEO
- Google Images can find your products
- Easier to organize

## ALT Text for Every Image (CRITICAL for SEO!)

### What is ALT Text?
Text description of image that helps:
- Visually impaired users (screen readers)
- SEO (Google ranks your images)
- Shows when image won't load

### ALT Text Formula:
`[Color] [Product Type] [Key Feature] [Angle/Context]`

### Examples:

**Main Product Image:**
```
Black wireless Bluetooth headphones with noise cancellation on white background
```

**Lifestyle Image:**
```
Woman wearing wireless headphones while exercising at gym
```

**Detail Shot:**
```
Close-up of padded ear cushion on wireless headphones showing soft memory foam
```

**Feature Image:**
```
Wireless headphones charging case showing USB-C port and LED battery indicator
```

### ALT Text Tips:
✅ Be specific and descriptive
✅ Include target keywords naturally
✅ Keep under 125 characters
✅ Describe what's IN the image
❌ Don't start with "Image of..." or "Picture of..."
❌ Don't keyword stuff
❌ Don't leave blank

## How to Take Great Product Photos at Home

### Equipment Needed:
- **Camera:** Smartphone (iPhone/Android is fine!)
- **Lighting:** Natural window light OR cheap LED lights ($20)
- **Background:** White poster board or sheet ($5)
- **Tripod:** Phone tripod ($15) - keeps images sharp

### Setup:
1. Place white background near window
2. Put product on background
3. Position camera on tripod
4. Take photos from multiple angles
5. Edit on phone or computer
6. Compress before uploading

### Smartphone Camera Settings:
- Turn ON grid lines
- Turn OFF flash
- Use portrait mode for product focus
- Use highest resolution
- Clean your lens!

### Lighting Tips:
- **Best Time:** Midday with indirect sunlight
- **Setup:** Place product 3-5 feet from window
- **Avoid:** Direct harsh sunlight (use sheer curtain)
- **Reflector:** Use white cardboard to fill shadows

## Image Optimization Workflow

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Take/Source Images**
   - Multiple angles
   - High resolution
   - Good lighting

2. **Edit Images** (if needed)
   - Remove background → Remove.bg
   - Crop/resize → Canva
   - Adjust brightness/contrast

3. **Resize to 2048x2048**
   - Use Canva or Photopea
   - Keep aspect ratio

4. **Compress Files**
   - Upload to TinyPNG
   - Reduce to 200-500KB
   - Download optimized versions

5. **Rename Files**
   - Use descriptive names
   - Include keywords
   - Use hyphens

6. **Upload to Shopify**
   - Add ALT text to each
   - Arrange in best order
   - Save product

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Too Small:** Blurry when zoomed
❌ **Too Large:** Slow page loading = lost sales
❌ **Wrong Aspect Ratio:** Weird cropping on mobile
❌ **Dark Images:** Can't see product details
❌ **No ALT Text:** Missing SEO opportunity
❌ **Generic Names:** IMG_1234.jpg
❌ **Inconsistent Style:** Looks unprofessional
❌ **Only 1-2 Images:** Not enough information
