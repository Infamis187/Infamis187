# Complete Shopify Dropshipping Setup Guide

Start earning commissions without holding inventory! This guide will get your dropshipping store running.

## What is Dropshipping?

**Simple Explanation:**
1. Customer buys product from YOUR store ($50)
2. You order from supplier at wholesale price ($30)
3. Supplier ships directly to customer
4. You keep the difference ($20 profit = commission!)

**No inventory needed!** You only buy products AFTER customers buy from you.

## Step 4: Import Products to Your Store

### Using DSers:

1. **Find Product on AliExpress**
   - Copy the product URL

2. **In DSers Dashboard:**
   - Click "Find Suppliers"
   - Paste AliExpress URL
   - Click "Add to Import List"

3. **Edit Product Details:**
   - Click "Import List" (top menu)
   - Click "Edit" next to product

   **Customize:**
   - ✏️ Title - Make it catchy & SEO-friendly
   - 💰 Price - Mark up 2-3x supplier price
   - 📝 Description - Use templates from PRODUCT_TEMPLATES.md
   - 🖼️ Images - Select best photos (or edit)
   - 🏷️ Variants - Choose colors/sizes to offer
   - 📦 Shipping - Select fastest option

4. **Push to Shopify:**
   - Click "Push to Shopify"
   - Product appears in your Shopify store!

**Repeat for 10-20 products to start.**

## Step 6: Automate Order Fulfillment

### How It Works:

1. **Customer Places Order**
   - Pays you $50 on your Shopify store

2. **Order Appears in DSers**
   - DSers detects new order automatically

3. **You Fulfill Order**
   - Click "Fulfill Orders" in DSers
   - Review order details
   - Click "Order Now"
   - DSers auto-fills customer info on AliExpress
   - You pay supplier $30

4. **Supplier Ships to Customer**
   - Ships directly to your customer
   - Tracking number sent to DSers
   - Customer receives email with tracking

5. **You Keep Profit**

### Semi-Automated Process:

**With DSers:**
- Auto imports product info ✅
- Auto-fills customer details ✅

## Step 7: Payment & Shipping Settings

### Set Up Shopify Payments:

1. **In Shopify Admin:**
   - Settings → Payments
   - Choose payment provider:
     - **Shopify Payments** (best, no transaction fees)
     - **PayPal** (backup option)
     - **Stripe** (alternative)

2. **Requirements:**
   - Business information
   - Bank account (for payouts)
   - Tax ID (if applicable)

3. **Activation:**
   - Usually instant approval
   - Some stores: 1-3 days review

### Configure Shipping:

1. **Go to Settings → Shipping**

2. **Create Shipping Zones:**
   - USA: Free shipping on $50+
   - International: $9.95 flat rate

3. **Shipping Rates:**

**Option A: Free Shipping (Recommended)**
```
All countries: FREE
(Build cost into product price)
```

**Option B: Flat Rate**
```
USA: $4.95
International: $9.95
```

**Option C: Tiered**
```
Orders under $50: $4.95
Orders $50+: FREE
```

4. **Processing Time:**
   - Set to "2-3 business days"
   - (Gives you time to order from supplier)

5. **Shipping Times:**
   - Set realistic expectations
   - "Delivery: 10-20 business days"
   - (AliExpress typically takes 15-25 days)

## Common Questions

### Q: How much money do I need to start?
**A:** Minimal! You can start with:
- Shopify: $1 for 3 months (new stores)
- DSers: FREE
- Domain: $14/year
- **Total: ~$15 to start**

You only pay suppliers AFTER customers buy from you!

### Q: How long until I make money?
**A:** Depends on marketing:
- Good marketing: Sales within 1-2 weeks
- No marketing: Could take months
- Average: First sale in 2-4 weeks

### Q: What profit margin should I aim for?
**A:**
- Minimum: 40% margin
- Good: 50-60% margin
- Excellent: 70%+ margin

Example: Sell for $40, cost $15 = $25 profit (62% margin)

### Q: Shipping takes too long! What do I do?
**A:**
- Use ePacket shipping (10-20 days vs 30-60)
- Consider Spocket for USA suppliers (5-10 days)
- Set customer expectations upfront
- Offer tracking numbers
- Send follow-up emails

### Q: What if customer wants refund?
**A:**
1. Try to resolve issue first
2. Contact supplier for solution
3. If needed, issue partial refund
4. Only full refund if absolutely necessary
5. Most suppliers won't accept returns (factor this into prices)

### Q: How do I handle customer service?
**A:**
- Respond within 24 hours
- Be honest about shipping times
- Provide tracking updates
- Offer solutions, not excuses
- 10% of revenue goes to handling issues (normal)
