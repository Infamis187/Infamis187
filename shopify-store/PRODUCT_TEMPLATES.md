## Quick SEO Tips for All Products

### Do This:
✅ Use keywords naturally 2-3 times
✅ Write benefits, not just features
✅ Include measurements & specifications
✅ Add emojis for visual appeal
✅ Create urgency with limited offers
✅ Highlight guarantees & free shipping
✅ Use bullet points for easy scanning
✅ Answer common questions

### Don't Do This:
❌ Keyword stuffing
❌ All caps text
❌ Generic descriptions
❌ Spelling errors
❌ Missing product details
❌ Ignore mobile users
❌ Forget call-to-action
