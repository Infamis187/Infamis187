## 1. DSers (AliExpress) ⭐ RECOMMENDED FOR BEGINNERS

### Overview:
Official AliExpress dropshipping app. Connect to millions of products from Chinese suppliers.

### Pricing:
- **Free:** 3,000 products, 100 orders/month
- **Basic ($19/mo):** 20,000 products, 500 orders/month
- **Advanced ($49/mo):** Unlimited products, unlimited orders
- **Pro ($499/mo):** Advanced features, bulk orders

### Pros:
✅ Completely FREE to start
✅ Millions of products
✅ Easy product import
✅ One-click fulfillment
✅ Good for testing products
✅ No upfront investment
✅ Official AliExpress integration

### Cons:
❌ Long shipping (15-30 days)
❌ Variable quality
❌ Chinese suppliers (language barriers)
❌ Packaging may have Chinese text
❌ Returns can be difficult

### Best Products:
- Phone accessories
- Jewelry
- Home gadgets
- Pet products
- Fashion accessories

### Installation Steps:

1. **In Shopify Admin:**
   - Apps → Shopify App Store
   - Search "DSers"
   - Click "Add app"

2. **Set Up DSers:**
   - Create account (free)
   - Link AliExpress account
   - Connect to Shopify

3. **Import Products:**
   - Browse AliExpress
   - Add to DSers import list
   - Edit details
   - Push to Shopify

### How to Make Money:
- Buy from supplier: $8
- Sell on your store: $25
- **Profit: $17 per sale**

**Time to First Sale:** 1-2 weeks with marketing

## 2. Spocket ⭐ RECOMMENDED FOR USA/EU

### Overview:
Dropship from USA and EU suppliers. Faster shipping, better quality.

### Pricing:
- **Free:** 25 products, Spocket branding
- **Starter ($29/mo):** 300 products, 25 premium products
- **Pro ($59/mo):** 1,000 products, 250 premium products, bulk checkout
- **Empire ($99/mo):** Unlimited, 10,000 premium products, branded invoicing

### Pros:
✅ USA/EU suppliers (fast shipping!)
✅ 2-7 day delivery
✅ Higher quality products
✅ No Chinese packaging
✅ Branded invoicing
✅ Better customer service
✅ Higher perceived value

### Cons:
❌ More expensive products
❌ Lower profit margins
❌ Fewer product options
❌ Not free (except limited plan)
❌ Premium products cost more

### Best Products:
- Fashion & apparel
- Beauty products
- Home decor
- Baby products
- Organic/eco products

### Installation Steps:

1. **Go to Spocket:**
   - Visit Spocket.co or Shopify App Store
   - Click "Start free trial"

2. **Connect Shopify:**
   - Sign up with email
   - Authorize Shopify connection

3. **Browse Products:**
   - Filter by "US/EU suppliers"
   - Look for "Premium" badge
   - Check shipping times

4. **Import to Store:**
   - Click "Add to Import List"
   - Customize pricing
   - Push to Shopify

### How to Make Money:
- Buy from supplier: $20
- Sell on your store: $45
- **Profit: $25 per sale** (better margins due to fast shipping)

**Time to First Sale:** 1-3 weeks

## 3. CJ Dropshipping ⭐ BEST VALUE

### Overview:
All-in-one dropshipping platform. Global warehouses, product sourcing, POD.

### Pricing:
- **FREE:** Unlimited products, pay per order
- No monthly fees!
- Optional: $50 recharge for faster processing

### Pros:
✅ Completely FREE
✅ Multiple warehouses (USA, China, EU)
✅ Product sourcing service
✅ Print-on-demand options
✅ Bulk orders
✅ Better packaging
✅ Can request custom products
✅ Lower prices than AliExpress

### Cons:
❌ More setup required
❌ Not as automated as DSers
❌ Longer initial setup
❌ Need to recharge account ($50 min)

### Best Products:
- Any niche
- Custom products
- Bulk orders
- Hard-to-find items

### Installation Steps:

1. **Sign Up:**
   - Visit cjdropshipping.com
   - Create account

2. **Install Shopify App:**
   - Shopify App Store → "CJ Dropshipping"
   - Install and connect

3. **Find Products:**
   - Search CJ platform
   - Or submit sourcing request
   - Import to Shopify

4. **Recharge Account:**
   - Add $50+ to CJ wallet
   - Used to pay for orders

### How to Make Money:
- Buy from CJ: $6 (cheaper than AliExpress!)
- Sell on store: $22
- **Profit: $16 per sale**

**Time to First Sale:** 2-3 weeks

## 4. Printful (Print-on-Demand)

### Overview:
Create custom products with YOUR designs. T-shirts, mugs, phone cases, etc.

### Pricing:
- **FREE:** No monthly fees
- Pay per order only
- No minimums

### Pros:
✅ 100% FREE
✅ Your own designs = unique products
✅ No inventory
✅ Fast USA shipping (3-7 days)
✅ High-quality printing
✅ Mockup generator
✅ Integrates perfectly with Shopify
✅ Great for branding

### Cons:
❌ Higher base costs
❌ Need to create designs
❌ Lower profit margins
❌ Competitive market
❌ Need design skills (or hire designer)

### Best Products:
- T-shirts ($13-$18 cost, sell $25-$35)
- Hoodies ($23-$30 cost, sell $45-$60)
- Mugs ($8-$10 cost, sell $18-$25)
- Phone cases ($10-$13 cost, sell $20-$30)
- Posters ($5-$8 cost, sell $15-$25)
- Tote bags ($9-$12 cost, sell $20-$28)

### Installation Steps:

1. **Create Account:**
   - Visit printful.com
   - Sign up free

2. **Install App:**
   - In Shopify: Apps → Printful
   - Connect your store

3. **Create Products:**
   - Choose product (t-shirt, mug, etc.)
   - Upload your design
   - Generate mockups
   - Set price
   - Add to Shopify

4. **Design Tips:**
   - Use Canva for designs (free)
   - Or hire designer on Fiverr ($5-$20)
   - Keep designs simple
   - Target specific niches

### How to Make Money:
- T-shirt cost: $15
- Sell for: $30
- **Profit: $15 per sale**

**Best For:** Building a brand, unique products

## Can You Use Multiple Apps?

**YES!** And you should:

**Example Strategy:**
- DSers: Test new products (cheap)
- Spocket: Best sellers (fast shipping)
- Printful: Branded items (unique)

**How It Works:**
- All apps connect to same Shopify store
- Different products from different suppliers
- Customer never knows the difference
